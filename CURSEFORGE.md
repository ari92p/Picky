# Picky - Rogue Lockpicking Helper (WoW Forever)

Picky makes lockpicking for other players fast and comfortable. Built for WoW Forever.

## Features

* **Right-click to pick:** click a locked box in your bags or in the trade window.
* **Duty mode:** finds players asking for a rogue in chat (English and German), invites them, greets them, thanks once for tips and leaves the group afterwards. Customers who invite you directly are accepted too. If someone you invited never joins but trades with you anyway, the order still counts.
* **Smart pop-ups:** a new request, a completed trade (click to thank and leave the group) and a box that is too high for you. Each one stays only while it is relevant, and you can pick the request sound in the settings.
* **Skill too high:** whispers that need a higher skill than yours get an automatic reply. The text is editable and uses the placeholders `{need}` and `{skill}`. Public chat is never answered.
* **Announcements** in trade chat with channel selection and the placeholders `{skill}`, `{pick}` and `{city}`. The game only allows channel posts from a click, so announce with Shift-click on the minimap button or the Announce button.
* **Order history** with boxes picked, tips and time spent.
* **Warning** when a customer's box needs a higher skill than yours.
* **Leveling guide** for lockpicking in WoW Forever.
* English and German interface, test mode with full simulation and a copyable log, built-in self tests.

## Getting started

Type `/picky` to open the window, then turn on Duty. That's it.
